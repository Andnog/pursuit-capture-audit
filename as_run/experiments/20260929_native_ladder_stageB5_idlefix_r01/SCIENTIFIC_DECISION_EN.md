# Decision — native ladder, stage B5 (idle pursuers off-lane, plan-only credit)

**Pre-registered primary: met** — pooled helper-removed Δ = +0.109, 95 % CI [0.020, 0.209], 46 contributing scenarios
(C1 +0.23 [0.04, 0.45]; C2 +0.15 [0.02, 0.36]; C3 n = 1; C4 −0.01; C5 −0.03).

**But the primary contrast is invalid as a test of coordination, found post hoc.** Under driver noise the roles swap:
in C2 s19 the "helper" P4 ends up in front and makes all 20 captures, so removing it removes the capturer. The contrast
then credits a single-pursuer capture as coordination — what D06 forbids.

**Post-hoc joint-versus-individual test (A-69)** on the same 46 plans and 20 seeds: Δ_joint = P(full plan) − max over
participants of P(that pursuer alone, others parked off-lane).

**Reading.** With a same-speed scripted evader and capture by blocking, the hand-built multi-pursuer plans do **not**
capture more often than their best single pursuer acting alone; in the corridor they do worse (pursuers interfere).
The B5 primary "pass" is therefore **not** evidence of coordination and is not carried forward.
