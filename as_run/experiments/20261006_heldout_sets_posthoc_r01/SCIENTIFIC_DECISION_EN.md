## Reading

1. **The controller advantage is smaller on HELD-OUT B than on TEST, by more than sampling error** (difference
   +0.081 [0.011, 0.149], uncorrected). The TEST figure overstated what a new set of cities would show. An honest
   summary of the held-out evidence is "positive in eight of eight cities, distinguishable from zero in four,
   between +0.06 and +0.15 pooled within a set".
2. **The advantage of PLB over the simpler junction ambush is a TEST result, not a general one.** On HELD-OUT B the
   junction ambush is close to PLB (0.267 against 0.291).
3. **The measurement gap is large in both sets and also differs between them** (+0.666 and +0.549). It is not a
   constant: it is smallest where proximity itself is rarely reached (Barcelona, 0.469).
4. **Under the 5 m rule the two controllers are indistinguishable in both sets.** This is the most stable result of
   the two evaluations.
5. **OC rates span 0.02 to 0.43 for the same pursuit team across cities.** Capture difficulty depends strongly on the
   network. The two centres with many one-way streets are the hardest for every controller. No cause was tested.

## Consequence

The article should present the two held-out sets together, state the difference between them, and stop describing
PLB as better than the junction ambush without qualification. The measurement claims need no change.
