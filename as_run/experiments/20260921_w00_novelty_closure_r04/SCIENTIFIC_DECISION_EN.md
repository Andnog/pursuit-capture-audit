## What this package found against itself first

B06 was recorded in r03 at `METHOD_SECTIONS` over a *"full 6-page PDF."* The document is **14 pages**. The
r03 reading came from page images of a render that stopped early. Two things were therefore missed.

**The one that matters:** B06's pursuit task **does define a joint capture predicate** — *k*-agent pursuit
requires at least *k* agents occupying *k* positions adjacent to the prey, within capture range *r* — and the
task explicitly requires that when far from the prey *"agents need to agree on and commit to go for the same
prey."* The r03 entry recorded only *"none; no bodies, lanes or collision geometry."* True, and incomplete.

So the earlier phrasing — that B06 has *no joint feasibility* — **overstated this project's position and is
withdrawn.**

## The difference is re-grounded, not withdrawn

B06's joint condition is a **cardinality count over grid adjacency**. On a grid, **any satisfiable count of
adjacent cells is physically realizable**, so counting is a *sound* team-feasibility certificate in that
domain. The lane-capacity witness shows counting becomes **unsound** the moment bodies and finite lane
capacity exist: a 14.0 m lane admits two 5.0 m bodies at a 2.5 m gap (12.5 m) but not three (20.0 m).

The claim is therefore about **when a count stops certifying feasibility**, not about the absence of a joint
condition. This is a narrower and more defensible statement than the one it replaces. B06 also carries **no
temporal representation** anywhere in its 14 pages — confirmed, not merely unrefuted.

## H2 is narrowed a second time, by a 1993 paper

Jennings (1993) defines **commitments** as *"pledges to undertake a specific course of action"* and
**conventions** as *"means of monitoring commitments in changing circumstances."* The `switch_margin`
hysteresis **is a convention in that sense.**

H2 previously conceded that *learned termination* is prior art (B06). It must now concede more: **commitment
held under a revision rule is prior art from 1993**, three decades before its RL formulation. What survives:

1. whether the committed **object** may be a jointly-feasible interception opportunity carrying
   interval-valued arrival sets, and
2. whether the **revision rule is learned rather than designed**.

Neither is addressed by the abstract, so the narrowed form stands. It is visibly smaller than what it
replaces.

## C03 and C04 are blocked by something definite

B06 reference [15] resolves to **Riedmiller & Withopf (2005)**, the **multi-option framework**: *"a
centralized model in which multiple agents are considered as a single meta-agent that chooses a joint option
o = (o₁,…,oₙ)"*, valued as `Q_joint(s_t, o_t)`, action space `|O|ⁿ`. This is the **closest structural relative
of C04** in the corpus.

It is recorded `SECONDHAND_METHOD_DESCRIPTION_NOT_A_READ`. **The question C04 turns on is exactly the one the
secondhand description cannot answer:** whether that joint option space is *filtered by a feasibility
predicate over the combination*, or merely enumerated and valued. If it is filtered, C04 is much weaker than
currently written.

**Four retrieval routes failed.** The publisher record is CLOSED, the **abstract itself is elided by the
publisher**, there is no open-access copy, and the author's own publication list links no PDF.
De Gruyter/Oldenbourg is not among the supplied proxies.

This is a **retrieval** failure, not a search failure, and is reported as one.

## Method finding

Every previous search step moved forward or sideways. Reading the **reference list of a source already read**
was tried once, here, and produced **two constraining records that thirty-five keyword searches did not**.
Both are **ancestors** — 1993 and 2005 — which is precisely why keyword search over recent literature and
forward citation traversal could not reach them.

**Backward citation chasing is now required** for every source read at method depth or deeper (A-33). This
adds work and relaxes no acceptance rule.
