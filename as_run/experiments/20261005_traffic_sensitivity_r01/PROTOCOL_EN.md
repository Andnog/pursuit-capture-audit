# PLB and pursuit with background traffic (A-127 T)

**Registered in:** `governance/AMENDMENTS_EN.md`, "A-127 addition", before any run of this probe and before any
outcome of the kinematic study had been read.

**Question.** Does the advantage of predictive lane blocking over pursuit, and the disagreement between proximity and
operational capture, hold when other vehicles use the network?

**Design.**
- Scenarios and seeds of the kinematic study: salt `PK1`, seven non-TEST cities, 20 scenarios × 2 SUMO seeds ×
  AVOIDANT/RANDOM × teams 3/5/7 × {PLB c2_15, pursuit}. 3,360 scheduled episodes.
- Background traffic of A-92: about 300 random-trip passenger cars per city, one departure per second, fixed seed per
  city (`assets/background_traffic`).
- The paired reference without traffic is arm `K0` of `experiments/20261005_kinematic_sensitivity_r01`.

**Primary.** PLB − pursuit with traffic, pooled over evaders and teams, family-clustered 95 % lower bound > 0.

**Secondary.** With traffic minus without, for each controller (paired); body-gap 5 m − OC for the pursuit team with
traffic; collisions.

**Scope.** One traffic level, generated by random trips. It is not calibrated demand, and background drivers do not
react to the pursuit. PLB's arrival-time estimates assume free flow.
