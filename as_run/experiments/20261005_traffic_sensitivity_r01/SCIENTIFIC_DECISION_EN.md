# Scientific decision — PLB and pursuit with background traffic (A-127 T)

**Status:** complete. Primary **met**.

## What was run

The scenarios and seeds of the kinematic study (salt PK1, seven non-TEST cities, AVOIDANT/RANDOM, teams 3/5/7,
20 scenarios × 2 SUMO seeds, PLB c2_15 and pursuit) with about 300 random-trip passenger cars per city. 3,360
episodes scheduled; 84 failed scenario placement, in the same 42 conditions as without traffic; 3,276 completed
(273 families). The paired reference without traffic is arm K0 of `experiments/20261005_kinematic_sensitivity_r01`.

## Results (OC unless stated; family-clustered 95 % intervals)

| | PLB | Pursuit | PLB − pursuit | City → family |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Without traffic (K0) | 0.439 | 0.313 | +0.126 [0.090, 0.164] | [0.079, 0.180] |
| With traffic | 0.377 | 0.283 | **+0.094 [0.060, 0.130]** | [0.050, 0.137] |

- **Primary:** lower bound 0.060 > 0, met.
- With traffic minus without (paired): PLB −0.062 [−0.095, −0.030]; pursuit −0.029 [−0.063, 0.004].
- Body gap 5 m − OC for the pursuit team with traffic: +0.638 [0.593, 0.682] (without: +0.619). Body-gap 5 m success
  of pursuit: 0.921 with traffic, 0.932 without.

## Reading

1. PLB keeps its advantage over pursuit when other vehicles use the network, and the disagreement between the 5 m
   rule and OC is unchanged.
2. Traffic costs PLB about 6 points of capture, and its advantage over pursuit is smaller with traffic (+0.094) than
   without (+0.126). The difference between those two differences was not registered and is not tested here.
3. PLB plans with free-flow arrival times. That is a plausible reason for its loss; it was not tested.

## Limits

One traffic level, generated by random trips with a fixed seed per city. It is not calibrated demand. Background
drivers do not react to the pursuit, and there are no signals priorities or emergency behaviours beyond SUMO's defaults.
