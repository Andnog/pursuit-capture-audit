# Post hoc note — is PLB's advantage smaller with traffic? (A-133, R4-C)

**Status:** post hoc, not registered. Computed by `scripts/posthoc_traffic_interaction.py` from the sealed results of
the kinematic (arm K0) and traffic packages, which are not modified.

The traffic probe reported PLB − pursuit as +0.094 with traffic and +0.126 without, and the article said the
advantage "is smaller on busy streets". The fourth review pointed out that the difference between those two
advantages had not been tested.

**Result.** (PLB − pursuit with traffic) − (PLB − pursuit without) = **−0.032**, family-clustered 95 % interval
[−0.066, 0.000]; city → family [−0.077, 0.012]; 273 families, 819 cells.

**Consequence.** The observed advantage is 3 points smaller with traffic, and the interval reaches zero. A smaller
advantage with traffic is therefore not established. The statements in the article, the supplement and the sealed
decision of the traffic probe (reading 2, "its advantage over pursuit is smaller with traffic") are to be read with
this qualification; the sealed text is not edited.
