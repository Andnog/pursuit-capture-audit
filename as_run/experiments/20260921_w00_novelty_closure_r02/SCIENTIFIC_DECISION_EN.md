# Scientific decision — citation chasing on B03, B04 and B06

Experiment: `20260921_w00_novelty_closure_r02`. Date: 21 September 2026.

**Decision: `FAMILY_OPEN; NO REFUTATION FOUND; C04 NARROWED TO KNOWN; COMPOSITE CLAIM UNCHANGED.`**

---

## 1. The planned check could not be run

The protocol specified **forward citation traversal** of the three records this project concedes as prior
art. No citation index was reachable: the available search returns ranked topical matches, not a citation
graph.

A **topic-proximity search** was substituted, and the family is recorded **`FAMILY_OPEN`**.

This matters more than it might appear. Citation traversal was chosen precisely because it is the check
**most likely to refute the claim**: the composite claim is the conjunction of what B03, B04 and B06
separately establish, so a work combining them would cite them. Topic search cannot enumerate citing works
at all — it can miss one entirely while returning unrelated matches.

The substituted search found nothing implementing the conjunction. **That is close to worthless as
evidence**, and recording it as "family closed, no refutation" would have been the convenient reading and
the wrong one: a weaker instrument's silence presented as a stronger instrument's finding.

## 2. What the substituted search did find

**DT-GAT** (Scientific Reports, 2026) — hierarchical graph-attention allocation over a dynamic
interceptor/target topology, with *"learnable edge-feature biases that embed spatial reachability and
interception urgency directly into attention computation"* and a Gumbel-Softmax head for differentiable
discrete assignment.

It **does not** implement the conjunction, failing four separate predicates:

| Predicate | DT-GAT |
|---|---|
| Interval-valued arrival sets | **No** — scalar reachability and urgency edge features |
| Joint, non-pairwise compatibility over a candidate combination | **No** — per-edge soft assignment |
| Learned persistence or commitment | **No** — re-allocation under topology change |
| Lane-constrained vehicles with bodies | **No** — free-flying drones |

**But it narrows claim C04 to `KNOWN`.** *Learned selection informed by reachability* is prior art. That is
the **fourth** component of the original claim conceded, after arrival-time coordination, nonconvex safe
intervals with geometry, and learned commitment/termination.

**Depth caveat.** The publisher page requires authentication (HTTP 303). These details come from indexed
secondary summaries, **not the primary text**, so the record carries the weaker label
`METHOD_DESCRIPTION_FROM_SECONDARY_SUMMARY`. The *concession* is made on that basis, which is the
conservative direction. A *distinction* from DT-GAT would require the primary text and is not claimed.

## 3. Where the claim now stands

Four components conceded as `KNOWN`, none of them claimed:

1. arrival-time coordination among pursuers (salvo guidance);
2. nonconvex safe intervals with agent geometry (continuous-time MAPF);
3. learned commitment and termination in multi-agent pursuit (dynamic termination);
4. **learned reachability-informed allocation (DT-GAT) — new in this revision.**

What survives is the **conjunction**, and it is now narrower than when W0 opened: interval-valued **joint**
compatibility over a complete candidate combination, with learned **persistence**, against a **reactive
evader**, under a **lane-constrained vehicle** contract with bodies.

Matrix: 28 rows — 9 `KNOWN`, 12 `POTENTIAL_DIFFERENCE`, 7 `PENDING`, **0**
`DOCUMENTED_TECHNICAL_DIFFERENCE`, **0** `EMPIRICALLY_VALIDATED`.

**The composite claim is unchanged at `POTENTIAL_DIFFERENCE`.** Four families remain open and the `r01`
rule requires all five.

## 4. An observation worth recording about the trend

Each search block has conceded a further component. That is not a failure of the searches; it is what an
honest novelty audit looks like when the proposed contribution is a conjunction of individually-studied
ideas. It also sharpens the risk the technical gap already names: **if enough components are conceded, the
conjunction may be an application rather than a contribution.**

The plan's instruction for that case is explicit — *"If the work is an application, call it an
application"*. That judgement is not due yet, because the remaining distinctness has never been tested
empirically. But the trend is recorded now rather than noticed later.
